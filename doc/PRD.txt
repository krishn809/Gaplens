PROBLEM STATEMENT: Digital Dark Spot (AI-Assisted Information Gap Detection Platform)

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Online decision-makers encounter scattered, contradictory, and out-of-date information. While traditional search retrieves links and summarizers condense visible text, neither highlights missing requirements ("Digital Dark Spots"). GapLens inverts this model: it establishes an expectation checklist, audits multi-source evidence, verifies every claim with verbatim quotes, detects conflicting terms, flags temporal staleness, and generates a prioritized verification checklist.

2. ARCHITECTURE
- Backend: Python 3 with FastAPI, Uvicorn, and high-performance string matching algorithms.
- Frontend: HTML5, CSS3 Glassmorphism UI, Vanilla JavaScript, Node.js proxy server.
- Database: In-memory structured knowledge base with normalized citation anchors.
- Theme: Clinic white (#f8f8ff) light theme, glassmorphism blur, and interactive cursor animations.

3. CORE WORKFLOW
- Demo Authentication: One-click authentication for Krishn Kushwaha into the glassmorphism dashboard.
- Heatmap Matrix: Bidirectional matrix of Expected Areas vs Sources (Covered, Dark Spot, Conflict, Outdated).
- Quote Verifier: Verbatim quote matching ensuring numeric accuracy and eliminating hallucinated drift.
- Contradiction Detection: Identifies cross-source discrepancies and evaluates source authority.
- Freshness & Staleness: Identifies references to prior cycles (e.g. Fall 2023) and flags expired dates.
- Verified Action Checklist: Ranked verification items with auto-drafted outreach inquiry emails.
- Re-Run Diff: One-click ingestion of official 2026 Policy Handbook demonstrating instant closure of dark spots.
